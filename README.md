# Study Rooms

Book a study room at the university library.

Students see which rooms are free at a chosen time, book one for up to two hours and cancel it
up to 15 minutes before it starts. Library staff block rooms for maintenance and export a weekly
usage report.

## Layout

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `src/main/java/rooms/` | Rooms, bookings and the booking rules (Java) |
| `notify/` | Emails students about their bookings (Go) |
